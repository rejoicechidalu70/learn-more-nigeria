# Learn More Nigeria

Build a web app called "LEARN MORE" - an education platform for Nigerian students, teachers, and skill learners. Tagline: "Learn anything. Anytime."

Mobile-first, lightweight, clean and friendly design with blue and green palette, supporting light and dark mode. Big buttons and clear navigation: Home, Practice, AI Tutor, Classes, Notes, Skills, and Profile/Dashboard.

Core features:
1. Past Questions & CBT Practice:
- Exams: NECO, WAEC, JAMB (UTME) with subject and year filters.
- Practice mode (with instant explanations) and Exam mode (timed JAMB-style CBT interface).
- Auto-scoring, breakdown by topic, bookmarks, and retry wrong answers.
- "Explain this answer" AI tutor feature.

2. AI Tutor:
- Chat interface for learning secondary and university topics with step-by-step explanations, simple English and Pidgin support, and interactive quizzes.

3. Online Classes & Catch-Up:
- Live/scheduled class schedules and recorded video archive with transcripts.
- Missed classes catch-up tracking on the dashboard.

4. Lesson Notes Library:
- Organized by Level (JSS, SSS, University) > Department/Subject > Course > Topic.
- Search, view, download, and teacher upload workflows.

5. Free Skills Academy:
- Tech tracks (Web Dev, Cybersecurity, AI, Video Editing, Graphic Design, Data Analysis, Mobile Dev, Digital Marketing) and Vocational tracks (Fashion Design, Catering/Baking, Makeup, Hair Styling, Shoe Making, Phone Repair, Photography, Barbing).
- Modules, lessons with video + text + quizzes, and completion certificates.

6. Unified Dashboard:
- Progress tracking, streaks, recent CBT scores, catch-up classes, and certificates.
